Your practice

Tummo, Box, coherence, sleep. Or your own.

Start with one of 19 guided techniques – or build your own pattern on the watch or iPhone and share it. Your watch guides you through every round.

+ Your own
Double tap

Stop the hold without opening your eyes.

  • You stay completely inside: eyes closed, focus within – and still end your hold to the second.
  • Every hold is recorded: the watch knows the exact moment – comparable round by round, over weeks.
  • A small turn, two taps: thumb and index finger, twice. Where double tap is not available, a long press does the same.

Every hold. Right on your wrist.

  • On the watch, straight after the session: rounds, breaths per round and every hold – with your best, your mean and the change from round to round.
  • A sentence that helps: where a round fell short of the plan – and what will carry the next one.
  • With a chest strap you see your pulse during the hold: in one of our own sessions it fell from 89 to 65 beats a minute in the first round – one example, not a benchmark.
  • The iPhone is the companion: the same analysis larger, your trend over the weeks – and the export.
How a round works
  1. 130 deep breaths

    The taps lead each breath in and let it go. You count nothing.

  2. 2The retention phase

    The watch counts in silence. When you need to breathe: turn your wrist slightly, then double tap – thumb and finger, twice.

  3. 3Recovery breath

    One deep breath in, hold it – the watch tells you when to let go. Then the next round.

Cold exposure: your body’s cold response
Cold exposure

Your body’s cold response. Session by session.

  • Cold shock: how far your pulse jumps when you enter the cold water.
  • Habituation: whether that jump gets smaller, compared with your earlier plunges – the measure cold-water studies use.
  • Cold score from 0 to 100, built from five parts: shock, time in the water, how fast your pulse returns, regularity and how it felt.
  • Water temperature and time under water on supported models such as Apple Watch Ultra, straight from the watch when it is submerged.
  • With a chest strap, every plunge beat by beat: pulse and HRV live on your wrist – as long as the strap stays above the water; Bluetooth does not reach through water. In the first 90 seconds the app also notices when a strong pulse rise and a deep drop come together, and suggests going in more slowly.
  • Progress over weeks: retention times, cold and heat sessions, your streak – and your weekly cold dose on the watch face.

  • Before the race, the talk, the negotiation: a clear rhythm, unnoticed on your wrist.
  • One swipe to the curve: the box as a line – you see where you are.
  • Make it yours: longer sides like 5-5-5-5 or 6-6-6-6, built on the watch or iPhone and shared.

Intuitive breathwork

Guided by touch, breath by breath.

The watch guides you with a language of taps, the same in all 19 techniques. Learn it once, then breathe with your eyes closed. If you lose the rhythm, the next signal tells you where you are.

How each phase feels
How much guidance? Three levels

The sounds only make audible what you feel on the watch – the guidance itself is the vibration on your wrist. Tap a sign or play the watch recording.

Why feel instead of look?

When you follow a vibration, your attention rests on what your body feels – not on a screen. Inhale, exhale, hold: you sense it on your wrist, and your eyes can stay closed.

Research has looked at this. In a study with 32 students breathing at 0.1 Hz with a handheld device, heart rate coherence was markedly higher when the guide could also be felt rather than only seen.

Bouny P. et al. (2023). Guiding Breathing at the Resonance Frequency with Haptic Sensors Potentiates Cardiac Coherence. Sensors 23(9):4494. doi:10.3390/s23094494 · Miri P. et al. (2020). PIV: Placement, Pattern, and Personalization of an Inconspicuous Vibrotactile Breathing Pacer. ACM TOCHI 27(1). doi:10.1145/3365107 · Research context, not measured by the app and not a promise of any effect.

Build & share

Build your breathing pattern. Share it with your community.

For coaches, teachers and everyone with a community: your pattern becomes a link – and everyone who opens it breathes in your rhythm.

  • Build – on the watch or on iPhone: phases, durations, rounds and haptic level, plus a name and a note.
  • Try it and fine-tune: breathe it right away, feel it, adjust – until it sits.
  • Share as a link or file: via WhatsApp, Telegram, Mail or AirDrop. Anyone with CARLON taps it and breathes along; everyone else reads in the message what they received.
  • Your name travels with it: coach name, tagline and your social link appear on the card your community sees when opening it – through every forward.
Refine your technique

Your watch listens to your breath.

In a quiet moment, CARLON lays your breath next to the pattern. You see where you are already in rhythm – and where it can become calmer still.

  • Target and actual in one picture: the pattern’s guide and the breath you really took, breath by breath.
  • Rhythm accuracy as a number: how precisely and how evenly you stay with a paced pattern such as box or coherence. In Tummo, which sets no pace, you see your own pace round by round instead – without a grade.
  • A gentle hint for next time – for example “0.4 s per cycle slower”.
How does the watch listen?
  • The watch’s microphone hears your breath. The sound is never stored.
  • Plus your pulse from the optical sensor, through every phase.

For research

Your breathing, as raw data.

One tap on iPhone builds a research archive – every session, ready for Kubios, Python or R.

  • Breath, recorded two independent ways: the breath wave from your chest as a raw signal with its sample rate, plus the breath onsets from the microphone. The app cross-checks the rate between them.
  • Every heartbeat: RR intervals in milliseconds as a text file and as a Kubios-ready CSV, each series with an SHA-256 checksum.
  • The whole session: phases, rounds, holds, sensor and firmware in one session.json – and one table across all sessions.
  • A methods text to paste: METHODS.md describes exactly what was recorded and how – including its limits.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Yes. CARLON is complete on the watch: guidance, double tap, heart rate, every session saved with its full analysis and your history – all without the iPhone nearby. The iPhone app adds data export and the charts in large. Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later, iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

What is the Cockpit?

The Cockpit is the data depth of a chest strap. Pair a Polar H10 or Movesense straight with your Apple Watch – no iPhone in between – and CARLON records every single heartbeat to the millisecond and the breath wave from your chest. That opens HRV beat by beat (RMSSD, SDNN, pNN50), the Poincaré plot, stress index, tachogram, DFA α1, cold beat by beat live on your wrist, and the research export with every heartbeat. The practice itself is complete without it: all 19 techniques, haptics, Tummo, the coach and every export run on the watch alone. The Cockpit is an optional in-app purchase, yearly or lifetime.

How do I get access?

CARLON Breathwork is in a closed beta through Apple TestFlight. Ask for access and we reply personally. The App Store release follows; the price is announced at launch.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you allow it. Finished sessions are saved as workouts, breathing sessions also as mindful minutes. Heart rate comes from the watch during the session.

Does any data leave my devices?

No account, no tracking, no ads, no third-party analytics. Sessions stay on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Data only leaves when you export it or share a pattern yourself.

How is CARLON different from the Mindfulness app on Apple Watch?

The Mindfulness app guides one adjustable breathing rhythm. CARLON brings 19 techniques with their own haptic language for every phase, Tummo rounds with timed holds, your own patterns to build and share, target and actual after every session – and, with a chest strap, every single heartbeat.

Is CARLON a medical device?

No. CARLON is a wellness app. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Intense breathing is not suitable with epilepsy, during pregnancy, with heart disease or uncontrolled high blood pressure – the app shows safety notes before every intense technique.

Which chest straps work?

Polar H10 and Movesense pair directly with your Apple Watch, no iPhone in between. Other chest straps can connect as long as they broadcast the standard Bluetooth heart-rate service; we have not tested them – pulse and beat intervals usually arrive, a breathing channel does not.

Can I breathe with my eyes closed?

Yes, that is the idea. Every phase has its own signal on your wrist – rising taps to breathe in, a falling tap to breathe out, a hum to hold. Once learned, you never need to look at the screen.

Can I build and share my own breathing patterns?

Yes. You set every phase on the watch and choose how strongly it taps. A pattern travels as a small file with its name and a readable text, so people without CARLON can follow it too.

Can I export my Apple Health data with Shortcuts?

Yes. CARLON brings Shortcuts actions that export Apple Health as JSON – workouts with all metrics, sleep per night, HRV split into sleep, day and training – plus all your CARLON sessions as structured data and every heartbeat of a chest strap recording as CSV for Kubios, Excel, R or Python.

Is intense breathing like Tummo safe?

Intense breathing techniques can cause dizziness or loss of consciousness. Practise only seated or lying down – never in water, not even a bathtub, never while driving or standing. CARLON asks you to confirm this before your first session. If you have a health condition or are pregnant, talk to your doctor first.

Which languages does CARLON speak?

English and German, on the watch and on iPhone.
