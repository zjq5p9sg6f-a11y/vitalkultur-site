The sensors

Polar H10 and Movesense Flash. Electrical, beat by beat.

Both read the heart's electrical signal at the chest – the standard for reliable HRV, where optical wrist and ring sensors fall short in motion. The app is tested with both, and they can run side by side in one study.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which sensors are supported?

Polar H10 chest straps (live and internal memory) and Movesense Flash sensors (live and in-sensor recording). The app is tested with these two sensors.

Does it work with Movesense?

Yes. Movesense Flash sensors stream heartbeats live and record in the sensor itself; the app also saves Movesense raw ECG and 9-axis motion data (accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer) as CSV. Movesense and Polar H10 can run side by side in one study.

How many sensors can record at once?

Up to four from one iPhone. They record to their own memory and are read out together afterwards; each recording is assigned to its participant with its real start time.

Can I use the data in Kubios HRV?

Yes. The CSV carries the exact settings for the Kubios custom text import in its header; the app also writes a Kubios text file and FIT. RR intervals are exported exactly as recorded.

How do I get values for each phase?

Tap an event button at each phase change. The segment table lists duration, beats, heart rate, RMSSD, SDNN and pNN50 for every phase between two events, computed with the same validated code as the rest of the app.

How is the app validated?

Every metric is compared with independent reference implementations (NeuroKit2, SciPy, Task Force definitions) on 18 RR series with 20,431 beats: 331 of 331 checks passed. Artifact detection matches NeuroKit2 beat by beat. The version-specific dossier lists every check and every error found and fixed.

Does any data leave the iPhone?

No. There is no account and no server. Recordings stay on your iPhone and in its regular backup, and go only where you export them.

Is there an Android version?

No – CARLON HRV Research runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The exported files open on any platform.

Is it a medical device?

No. It is a research and wellness tool and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

What does it cost?

A one-time purchase on the App Store – no subscription, no per-participant fees. Institutions can buy licenses in volume through Apple School Manager or Apple Business Manager.
