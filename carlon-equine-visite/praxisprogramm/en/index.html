Back to your practice software

Visite does not issue invoices. You pass a day’s services to your practice software as CSV and bill there as usual. Format: semicolon, UTF-8, one row per visit. The column names are German. Whether your software reads this file depends on its import function; the check above only covers the direction into Visite.

  • Daily service list: date, arrival, departure, horse, patient no. (from your export), owner, place, treatment (min), drive (km), drive (min), triage, actual/estimate.
  • Daily mileage: date, owner, horses, share, note; with the German fee schedule (GOT § 10) split by owner.
  • Per horse also JSON and FHIR R5 with measurements and raw data.

For practice software vendors

Visite reads exports from practice software itself and maps the columns. If you want to offer an export for Visite, these columns work best, semicolon and UTF-8:

Patienten-Nr.;Pferd;Rasse;Halterin;Telefon;E-Mail;Stall;Straße;PLZ;Ort;UELN;Mikrochip
10234;Sample horse;Hanoverian;Anna Muster;+49 170 0000001;;Musterhof;Hofweg 3;12345;Musterstadt;276098765432109;276098106543210
  • Available today: patient list as CSV into Visite (this page); back out the daily service list and mileage as CSV, per horse JSON and FHIR R5. There is no interface between the programs yet.
  • Repeated import: Visite recognises existing horses by patient number, UELN, microchip or name with owner and only fills empty fields; nothing is created twice or overwritten.
  • Visite also recognises English column names (horse, owner, phone, street, city …), first and last name in separate columns, comma or tab separators and Windows-1252.
  • The patient number stays with the horse, so the service list can be matched to the patient again in your software.
  • Due dates for vaccination, deworming and dental checks are imported when they are dates in their own column.
