The difference that arises before the measurement

A horse that has just been transported is not measuring itself.

Heart rate variability is a measure of recovery. Loading, the journey, an unfamiliar hall, unfamiliar people — all of it changes exactly the quantity you want to assess. The analysis we use explicitly requires at least seven minutes of rest in familiar surroundings. A measurement taken after arrival at the clinic systematically fails that condition.

Its own box, not an examination room

The owner puts on the belt, the horse stands as it always does. No transport, no excitement from strangers, no bulky equipment in the way.

You get the recording, not the appointment

The recording sits in the cockpit when you have time. You decide afterwards whether and when a visit is needed — and you see beforehand how urgent it is.

Repeatable without anyone driving out

A follow-up in two weeks costs nobody a drive. That is exactly how the trend comes about that a snapshot can never deliver.

Why the numbers hold

In horses, pauses are normal. Count them in and you measure nonsense.

A resting horse shows AV-block pauses — physiological, not a finding. Count them in raw and SDNN and RMSSD rise dramatically, and the markers that respond to small values flip into the opposite.

The same filter chain as in the app, not a rebuilt one

Five stages, AV-block detection with spline correction, motion artefacts, plausibility limits. We ported them from the original and checked value by value against the compiled version, bit pattern for bit pattern.

What difference it makes — tested on twenty resting recordings

Without this stage, the airway classification came out differently in eight out of twenty recordings, every time too conspicuous. With it: not a single difference from the app's analysis remains.

And where it is not enough, it says so

Too few beats, too short, too many artefacts — then it says “insufficient data” and gives no verdict. A tool that always says something is not a tool.

What a snapshot cannot do

The direction is the information.

A single HRV value in a horse says little — there is no robust normal value to measure it against. It only becomes meaningful against the animal's own history.

Over weeks rather than minutes

Every new recording joins the line. You see whether the markers improve, hold or deteriorate — with the time window, the number of measurements and the change as a figure.

Visible after a change

Housing, feeding, management, convalescence: the follow-up measurement costs no drive, so it actually happens. As an observation — the assessment stays with you.

Limits we draw ourselves

What CARLON deliberately does not do.

No diagnosis

The software shows measured values, signal quality, trend and deviation. The medical interpretation remains entirely with you. CARLON Equine Visite is not a medical device within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2017/745.

No treatment recommendation

Even when a trend looks unambiguous: whether anything is changed appears nowhere on the screen. That is your decision, not a metric's.

No database at our end

Your patients live in your device's storage. We have no access and could not delete them either. What passes between owner and practice is encrypted and unreadable to us.

Not an emergency channel

A recording is not a phone call. In acute cases what always applies still applies — act veterinarily, immediately.

Frequently asked

What vets ask us first.

Does CARLON replace an examination?

No. CARLON prepares trend data and helps with the order of visits. Whether a clinical examination, further diagnostics or treatment is needed is decided by you alone.

What does the owner need?

A commercially available chest belt and an Android device or a computer with Chrome or Edge. On iPhone and iPad, Apple does not permit Bluetooth in the browser; the page directs you to a capable device there.

What happens after cancellation?

The application switches to a reduced mode. Your data stays on the device and can still be opened and exported. We could not delete it — it is with you.

Do I need a data processing agreement?

The cockpit runs entirely in your browser; patient data never reaches us. For the encrypted transport path between owner and practice we are currently having the classification clarified by a lawyer and will tell you the outcome before you need it.
