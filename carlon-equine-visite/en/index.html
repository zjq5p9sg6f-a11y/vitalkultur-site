Why the numbers hold

Long pauses between beats are detected – and reported separately.

Heart rate variability is associated with the activity of the autonomic nervous system — the interplay of sympathetic and parasympathetic tone. It is therefore not a readout of a state: there is no robust normal value in the horse. That is why the deviation levels rest on the horse's own history. Only the breathing-pattern profile additionally compares with fixed limits — and those are derived from the group means of a study, not taken from it (Nyerges-Bohák 2025).

In a resting recording — 642 beats, 0.31 % artefact rate, rated excellent by the quality indicator — two beats, an AV block with its short follow-on beat, are enough to lift RMSSD from 24.2 to 55.5 ms. 130 % too high — and the quality indicator never warns. A high share of plausible beats says nothing about whether the metric is right.

The method in detail: filter chain, breathing-pattern profile, limits

A resting horse often shows long pauses between two beats — frequently a second-degree AV block; only an ECG shows what it is. Count them in raw and SDNN and RMSSD rise dramatically, and the measures that respond to small values flip into the opposite.

No metric without signal quality and artefact rate

AV-block pauses, motion artefacts, implausible beats — the analysis detects and corrects them before any metric is computed. Whether a recording comes from the app or from the browser station: you get the same, cleaned value. And where a file demonstrably did not pass through the chain, the report says so — those are raw values, and the report states it instead of claiming a correction that never happened. Implausible outliers are flagged, not taken over

Unfiltered, a resting horse looks systematically more conspicuous than it is. An overview that shows deviations too often is as worthless as one that shows none — so the data is cleaned first, compared second. So that a deviation stands out because it lies in the horse. Not in the measurement.

If signal quality or resting time falls short, it says so

Too few beats, too short, too many artefacts — then it says “insufficient data” and gives no verdict. A tool that always says something is not a tool.

A single HRV value in a horse says little — there is no robust normal value to measure it against. It only becomes meaningful against the animal's own history.

The difference that arises before the measurement

Resting HRV only exists where rest exists: in the horse’s own stable.

Transport, unfamiliar surroundings, unfamiliar people — each of these shifts exactly the autonomic balance you want to assess. For resting HRV the place of recording is not a side issue but part of the method: the analysis requires at least seven minutes of rest in familiar surroundings — below that it gives no result. After loading and a drive this condition is systematically not met — in the horse’s own box it is met every day.

  • Transport lowers resting HRV by about a third. RMSSD fell during road transport from 74 to 45 ms (3.5 h) and from 90 to 59 ms (8 h). Schmidt et al. 2010, Hormones and Behavior
  • Horses differ more from each other than from day to day. Variability between horses was about 40 % higher for heart rate and about 100 % higher for HF power than within the same horse – hence the comparison with its own baseline. Ohmura et al. 2012, American Journal of Veterinary Research
Why exactly there: protocol and comparability

Same box, same time, same protocol. Only under familiar conditions are two recordings comparable. At home, repeated over weeks, this builds a trend that shows how this horse’s resting HRV develops after a change in housing, feeding or training — the interpretation stays with you.

More on the measuring station: data path, file, privacy

Your client experiences your practice, not our software

She records via your measuring link on your recommendation, and the reply carries your letterhead. Every recording between two visits becomes a touchpoint of your practice.

Raw and corrected value side by side

In horses, physiological pauses — second-degree AV block is normal in a resting horse — and genuine artefacts occur in the same series. A filter cannot tell them apart; it discards both. The station therefore reports the raw value and the one over unremarkable pairs. Which share comes from what is your call, not the software's.

Nothing passes through us

The moment your client opens your measuring link, the file is already encrypted against your key on her device. She sends it by whatever route she chooses. We never see it and store it nowhere — in our assessment, therefore, no data processing agreement is needed between us.

Limits we draw ourselves

What CARLON deliberately does not do.

No diagnosis

The software shows measured values, signal quality, trend and deviation. The medical interpretation remains entirely with you. CARLON Equine Visite is not a medical device within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2017/745.

No treatment recommendation

Even when a trend looks unambiguous: whether anything is changed appears nowhere on the screen. That is your decision, not a metric's.

No database at our end

Your horse data lives in your device's storage. We have no access and could not delete it either. The owner's recording never passes through us at all: she sends you a file — opened via your measuring link, it is encrypted for your practice from the very first second.

Not an emergency channel

Frequently asked

Questions from practice – answered briefly.

Does CARLON replace an examination?

No. CARLON prepares trend data and helps with the order of visits. Whether a clinical examination, further diagnostics or treatment is needed is decided by you alone.

What does the owner need?

A Polar H10 chest belt and an Android smartphone or tablet, or a laptop with Chrome or Edge. On iPhone and iPad, Apple does not permit Bluetooth in the browser; the page directs you to a capable device there.

What happens after cancellation?

The application switches to a reduced mode. Your data stays on the device and can still be opened and exported. We could not delete it — it is with you.

Do I need a data processing agreement?

No. The cockpit runs entirely in your browser, and the owner's recording never reaches us either: her measuring station creates a file on her own device, which she sends to you herself. There is no relay at our end — and therefore nothing a data processing agreement could be about.

How good can a layperson’s recording be?

Every recording carries its own measurement report: share of usable beat pairs, artefact rate, sensor, timing resolution. A recording that carries nothing is flagged as exactly that — you never interpret blind. And the raw data comes with it: every single beat interval.

What is the breathing-pattern profile based on?

On a published comparison study (Nyerges-Bohák 2025, Equine Veterinary Journal, n = 40) that reports group differences in SD2, SDNN, TINN and SD2/SD1 — it contains no threshold value. Our comparison limits sit between its group means; that is our own derivation, not a value from the literature, and every limit is listed individually in the methodology. The profile describes a measurement pattern — it establishes nothing and replaces no examination.

Does this create a duty to keep watching?

No. CARLON sends no alerts, and the owner sees no classification — it only comes into being in your cockpit, when you open it. A file sent to you is a delivery like an email: when you review it is up to you, communicated to your clients as before. For emergencies, what has always applied still applies: the phone call.

Where does the baseline come from?

From this horse's own resting recordings: from three recordings within 30 days, the value counts as an individual baseline. Until then the cockpit openly shows that the comparison value still comes from the reference dataset — and how many of the horse's own recordings are still missing. It does not pretend to know something it does not.

Where is my data, and how do I back it up?

Only in your browser on your device – no cloud, no server of ours. Under Documentation, “Back up” creates a backup file; “Restore backup” brings everything back on a new device or browser, including your activation. If a recent backup is missing, the cockpit shows it at the top. On the clinic plan the records are also kept in the clinic folder on your network drive.

Can I charge for reviewing a recording?

You decide that under the German fee schedule for veterinarians (GOT) – there is currently no dedicated GOT item for telemedicine. CARLON delivers measurements, trends and a measurement report; whether this becomes a consultation and how you charge for it is up to you. Reviewing does not replace an examination.
